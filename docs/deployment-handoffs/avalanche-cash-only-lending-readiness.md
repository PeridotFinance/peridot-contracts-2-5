# Avalanche cash-only USDC/WAVAX lending: readiness report

Status: evidence summary for an operator decision (revised after review: reserve gate corrected to UNMET; margin-hooks-unset fixture added). **No launch approval is implied, no parameter or cap is proposed or changed, borrowing stays paused.** Draft. Nothing signed, broadcast or deployed.

This report is about ONE product. It is deliberately separate from the margin-trading work and from the vault work, and it uses product names because the track letters are not reconciled between the handoff files and the PR documents:

| Product name used here | What it is | Handoff 09 label (unreconciled) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Cash-only lending** | Fresh controller with two plain markets, pUSDC and pWAVAX, all cash stays local, no strategy | track B |
| AVAX margin trading | Legacy isolated margin through the Pharaoh adapter | track C |
| Pharaoh vaults | The two existing Pharaoh USDC / WAVAX vaults | track A |
| Pharaoh allocation | Paired strategy behind the markets (not built) | track D |

**The funded-sizing work does not change cash-only lending.** Ordinary liquidations use `PErc20.liquidateBorrow` and the controller; none of the contracts changed in the funded-fallback or funded-sizing work (margin config, margin account, margin liquidator, margin risk engine) are deployed or called by a cash-only deployment. Funded sizing also does not select a margin venue or approve a funding programme.

## 1. What a cash-only deployment contains (and what it does not)

Controller (`Unitroller` + `PeridottrollerAvalanche`), plain `PErc20Delegate` markets pUSDC and pWAVAX, `GuardedAvalanchePriceOracle` over `AvalanchePriceOracle`, interest rate models, borrowing paused at deployment. It needs no margin stack. All parameters in the tests (collateral factors 60% WAVAX / 75% USDC, rate model, reserve factor, caps) are **placeholders for measuring**, not recommendations (operator decision 3 in handoff 09).

## 4. Open items that block treating this product as ready

0. **Local borrowing reserve: mechanism implemented in the draft, unaudited; floor values and the audit are open (gate not met for launch).** See section 5.
1. **Parameters are undecided** (rate models, reserve factors, supply/borrow caps, collateral factors, cash target). The bad-debt-under-gap results make the CF/cap/utilisation choice material, not cosmetic.
2. **Lending bad-debt policy.** Measured: the plain markets have **no write-off**; an unpaid borrow stays on the books, the exchange rate does not move, and the loss surfaces as a first-come-first-served exit shortfall borne by the last suppliers of the debt asset (= max(0, loss - reserves in that market)). Reserve-factor interest is too slow to be the backstop (1.2% to 2.7% of borrows per year; about 19 years for one -50% gap). Tested backstops without new code: treasury `_addReserves`, treasury `repayBorrowBehalf`. A write-off / loss-socialisation mechanism would be new code and is not written. Funding separation: `avalanche-funding-purposes-and-loss-reserve.md`.
3. **Liquidator operations**: who runs it, with what budget in which asset, how it is monitored, what happens when it is empty.
4. **Oracle operations**: choose `maxAge` per feed; decide the availability trade-off (entered suppliers cannot redeem during a stale-feed window); who may change feed configuration; how feed changes are timelocked.
5. **Deployment script written and rehearsed on a LOCAL FORK only** (`avalanche-cash-only-deployment-rehearsal.md`): every parameter is an explicit input with no default, borrowing stays paused, collateral factors zero, margin hooks unset, read-only verifier. Not a deployment authorization; the handover to governance, the production parameter values and any real broadcast remain separate operator-signed steps.
6. **PERIDOT token decision: UNRESOLVED.** `PeridottrollerAvalanche` requires an existing contract at construction (the base hardcodes another chain's token). The deployment script requires `PERIDOT_TOKEN` as an explicit input and never deploys or defaults one; the rehearsal uses a labelled test-only stand-in. With all reward speeds at zero no claim path moves tokens (tested).
7. ~~No test runs lending with the margin hooks unset~~ **Closed by the dedicated fixture** (section 2): borrow, repay, redeem, ordinary liquidation, stale-feed rejection and cash exhaustion pass with both hooks at `address(0)` and no margin stack. Still open: the same fixture on the real deployment script (none exists).
8. **Storage layout diff** of `PeridottrollerAvalanche` against the live proxy layout is recorded as review reasoning; a `forge inspect` diff in CI is not part of any gate yet.
9. **Handoff files unreconciled.** The PR variant of handoff 00 (with section 6a) and the attached 00 are not reconciled; liquidity-policy rules in 6a may change the gate wording. No handoff 00 was edited.

## 5. Local borrowing reserve: borrow cash floor (IMPLEMENTED IN THE DRAFT; unaudited; no production value)

**Requirement (handoff 09 section 3b).** A per-market local borrowing reserve applies to NEW borrows and margin opens. It must never block repayment or authorized recovery: repay and repay-on-behalf always allowed (they add cash); normal voluntary close and normal liquidation that redeem pTokens may use reserved cash down to zero; the in-kind recovery paths are unaffected; ordinary supplier withdrawals are limited by available cash as usual, not by the reserve. Each path is tested "at and below the reserve". The reserve size ("cash target and borrowing reserve per market") is operator decision 3 and stays open.

**Three different things (kept distinct).**

| | Borrow cash floor (this section) | Accounting reserves (`totalReserves`, reserve factor) | Liquidator funding |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Purpose | limits how much cash NEW borrows may remove from the market, so exits and recoveries keep cash | the protocol's share of interest, a claim on the market's assets | debt-asset inventory a liquidator needs to repay debt |
| Where held | nowhere: it is a threshold on the market's own cash, enforced in `borrowAllowed` | inside the market (`totalReserves`) | the liquidator's own wallet |
| Funded by | suppliers' cash simply not being lent out | accrual from borrower interest | the liquidator operator |
| Protects | withdrawability / recovery cash against new borrowing | against losses, if sized for it | liquidations happening at all |
| Cannot protect against | bad debt, withdrawals by suppliers themselves (they may take cash below the floor), an unfunded liquidator | losses larger than the reserve; it is not cash reserved for withdrawals | oracle failure, insufficient collateral |

**Implementation (draft).** `PeridottrollerAvalanche` adds `borrowCashFloor(pToken)` (0 = disabled), the admin-only `_setBorrowCashFloor(pToken, floor)` (listed markets only, event `NewBorrowCashFloor`) and overrides `borrowAllowed`: it runs the original function first (pause, listing, price, cap, liquidity, margin hook, flywheel) and, only if that returns 0, requires `cash >= amount && cash - amount >= floor`, otherwise reverts "borrow cash floor". Consequences, all tested: with the floor at 0 behaviour is identical to the pre-floor controller (differential through a live implementation switch against a frozen copy, and a legacy-to-new-to-legacy upgrade round trip with state preserved); "borrow is paused" and every error code keep precedence; the floor can only turn an allowed borrow into a revert. The base `Peridottroller.borrowAllowed` changed from `external` to `public virtual` (no behaviour change). **Storage:** the mapping lives in a namespaced (ERC-7201 style) hash slot, so the wrapper adds no linear storage (checked: `tools/check-controller-storage-layout.sh` compares it with the base layout; a test shows no linear slot changes when floors are set) and a future base that appends storage cannot collide with it.

**Evidence (all on the pinned fork).** Cash-only suite (`AvalancheCashOnlyBorrowFloorFork.t.sol`, 9 tests, no margin stack): both markets at the maximum, one unit above, one unit below, exactly at the floor (next borrow of 1 unit rejected), already below by raising the floor, and below because suppliers withdrew; precedence of pause / price error / insufficient liquidity; floors never unpause; non-borrow paths from the same below-floor state compared with the floor active and disabled and found identical (repay, supplier withdrawal within cash and not beyond, ordinary liquidation in both directions); admin-only, listed-only, event, getter; storage slot; differential against the pre-floor controller; live upgrade and rollback. Margin suite (`AvalancheBorrowFloorMarginFork.t.sol`, 2 tests, kept separate): a long and a short margin open are rejected one unit under the floor and allowed at it, and below the floor margin close, flash liquidation and funded in-kind liquidation behave identically with floors active and disabled. Mutation checks: an off-by-one in the comparison is caught by the boundary and margin-open tests; checking the floor before the original checks is caught by the precedence test. **All floor values in the tests are SYNTHETIC.**

**Limits.** Not audited. The floor is checked against the market's current cash, which suppliers' withdrawals can lower below the floor at any time (by design). It does not stop a borrower from borrowing up to the floor and does nothing about bad debt. The admin can lower it, so the intended deployment puts the admin behind a timelock (not part of this repository's scripts). Whether a guardian may raise it quickly is an open choice.

## 6. Stale-price liquidation blocker (primary oracle only; secondary oracle deferred)

Decision applied: the secondary oracle (API publisher, fallback oracle, paid provider) is **deferred**; completed research stays in the repository but no further integration work is done. Cash-only lending uses the existing primary oracle: Chainlink feeds behind `GuardedAvalanchePriceOracle` (freshness check, zero/negative/future/mismatched/reverting rounds give the zero sentinel, the emergency price is never used, every consumer fails closed).

**The blocker that remains, stated explicitly.** While a feed is older than its configured max age, the guarded oracle returns 0 and: new borrowing is rejected (`PRICE_ERROR`), **ordinary liquidation is rejected** (so unhealthy accounts cannot be liquidated until a fresh round), and suppliers who entered markets cannot redeem or transfer claims even without debt (repay still works). Tested: `testStaleFeed_EmergencyPrice_RejectsBorrowAndLiquidation_RepayStillWorks`, `testStaleFeed_accountClassMatrix`, `testInKindClaim_TransferWithoutCash_AndWithDebtChecks` (stale part). This is the intended fail-closed behaviour, and it is an activation blocker for credit risk, not a defect: during an outage, bad debt can build up unliquidated, then be realised at the first fresh price.

**Measured feed cadence on mainnet (public node, 2026-11-03).** AVAX/USD: 80 rounds over 2.3 h, median 121 s, max 136 s between rounds. USDC/USD: strict 24 h heartbeat (rounds 86,401 to 86,467 s apart). Consequences for the max ages (an operator decision; the fork tests and the Fuji-derived defaults use 1,200 s and 90,000 s):
- AVAX 1,200 s is about 10x the observed cadence: ample today, but an exchange-driven quiet period or a delayed round longer than 20 minutes blocks liquidation.
- **USDC 90,000 s leaves only about 3,500 s (about 1 hour) of slack over the 24 h heartbeat.** A USDC round delayed by more than that makes USDC unpriced: **borrowing and liquidation in BOTH markets stop** (every account touches USDC or prices against it). A longer max age shrinks that blocker but lets a stale USDC price (at a depeg) persist longer; the trade-off is for the operator to choose and is not chosen here.

**What is kept, what is not.** Kept: freshness check, guarded zero sentinel, no emergency price, fail-closed consumers, execution-price checks of the margin swap module (margin is paused). Not implemented on Avalanche: an issuer-pause / blacklist check for native USDC (Circle's token exposes `paused()` and blacklisting; no consumer reads them). Not solved by anything in the draft: liquidation during a feed outage. Mitigations that stay inside the primary oracle: monitor feed age against max age and alert before it trips (monitoring list in the risk proposal), keep a pause-borrowing runbook, size reserves and collateral factors for gap risk during an outage window (risk proposal). **Deployment readiness and permission to activate borrowing are separate**: this blocker is one of the conditions for the second, not for the first.

## 7. Optional in-kind emergency exit (accepted by the operator): what is verified

For cash-only lending the in-kind exit is the supplier's **claim** (pTokens): it can be moved to another account without market cash, subject to the controller's collateral check when the account has debt. Verified in `testInKindClaim_TransferWithoutCash_AndWithDebtChecks`: with pUSDC cash exhausted a cash redemption fails but the claim transfers (the recipient redeems for cash once repayments restore cash); an account with debt cannot move all its collateral claims (refused by the collateral check) and can move a part that keeps it solvent; a stale feed blocks claim transfers by accounts that entered markets. It is **not** converted into the originally deposited asset and nothing promises conversion. For the Pharaoh vaults the in-kind exit (`redeemInKind`: both legs of the position, no swap) is verified by the `GATE|` tests R1 to R6 (`avalanche-track-a-launch-gate.md` section 12), with the open fairness problem F-IK-2 (`avalanche-vault-entry-fairness.md`): in-kind exits pay real tokens while deposits mint shares against a haircut NAV.

## 8. What this report does not say

- It does not say lending is safe at any particular cap, collateral factor or utilisation.
- It does not say borrowing may be unpaused. The tested state is "paused at deployment"; unpausing is a separate decision gated on the funded liquidator, monitoring and the local borrowing reserve.
- Nothing in the margin work (capacity matrix verdicts, funded fallback, funded sizing) is a precondition for or evidence about this product other than the shared oracle and controller tests listed in section 2.
