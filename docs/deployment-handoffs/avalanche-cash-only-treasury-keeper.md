# Cash-only pilot: treasury keeper (build step 2) — reviewable

Status: **DRAFT, for the operator's review.** Built and tested on a local fork only. **No live run, no real key, nothing signed or broadcast on any network**; no launch approval implied. The keeper's tests use a throwaway Foundry keystore created inside the test run and deleted afterwards. The liquidator executor (step 3), the operator setup steps (4) and Telegram (5) are **not started**.

## 1. What it is (operator decision 2026-11-06)
A **third hot wallet** that holds the protection USDC and executes `repayBorrowBehalf(account, type(uint256).max)` on pUSDC **itself** when the monitor's rule returns `DEFAULT_EXHAUSTED`. The Avalanche Safe is the **cold reserve that funds it**. It is the only execution path; there is no manual Safe-signing path for repayments any more (the Safe tooling becomes a top-up helper, not built yet). Code: `operator/src/keeper.ts` (logic), `operator/src/signer.ts` (external signer abstraction, first implementation: Foundry keystore), wired into `operator/src/main.ts` for `run` only (`check` never pays).

## 2. The rule is the monitor's, unchanged; the keeper only adds restrictions
Same fail-closed verdicts, same reference price (Uniswap V3 0.05% 30-minute TWAP), same 500 bp tolerance gate. Before any transaction:
1. **Confirmation** (the "persistence hardening" of treasury document 2.3.1, now implemented): `DEFAULT_EXHAUSTED` on `confirmEvaluations` (default 2) evaluations on distinct blocks spanning at least `confirmSeconds` (default 60) of chain time. Any other verdict, a blind cycle, or an RPC failure starts it over.
2. **Re-verification at execution**: a fresh full evaluation at the latest block must read `DEFAULT_EXHAUSTED` again, with a healthy reference price (a stale or zero oracle, a divergence, a missing reference or low pool liquidity all refuse).
3. **Amount bounded by the debt at execution**: the allowance is exactly the debt read now plus `allowanceMarginBp` (default 10 bp, interest until inclusion), never more; the sentinel repay pulls at most that; `repayBorrowBehalf` is **simulated** after the approval and before it is sent.
4. **Daily spend cap** (rolling 24 h, `keeper.dailySpendCapUsdc`, no default), the wallet must cover the whole debt (**no partial repayment**: that stays a human decision), fee cap (`maxFeeGwei`), AVAX gas floor, signer address must equal `treasury.address`, chain id 43114.
5. **Read-back after the payment**: the `RepayBorrow` event (payer, borrower, amount), the keeper's USDC delta equal to the event, debt of the account 0, allowance back to 0 (a reset transaction if a margin is left).
6. **Alerts**: a message on every payment (with the read-back), a critical alert whenever a confirmed default is *not* paid and why, escalation timers 1 h / 4 h / 24 h from the monitor.
7. **Halt** on anything unexpected after a transaction was sent (revert, read-back mismatch, an exception, an interrupted payment found at start-up): no further payment until a human clears `keeper.halted` / `keeper.pending` in the state file. After a failure that cost gas, a 300 s per-account cooldown prevents a polling loop from spamming approvals.
8. **Key-theft tripwire** (monitor): USDC leaving the keeper wallet without a recorded keeper payment is a critical `treasury:unexplained-outflow` finding.

## 3. Tests: the keeper against the 17-row truth table (fork, block 99,200,000)
`operator/test/fork/truth-table.fork.test.ts` (30 fork tests in two files, all pass; `fork-tests.txt`). The keeper runs after the monitor at **every stage** with a real Foundry-keystore signer on the local fork:
- **All 16 non-default rows (including the tail rows before liquidation, the partially liquidated and the untouched tail accounts; stale and zero-price rows through the hostile-state tests below): the keeper sends no transaction at all** (nonce stays 0).
- **The default row (exhausted account): first sighting -> waits; after 61 s -> pays exactly that account**: 3 transactions (approve, repay, approve 0), the keeper's USDC fell by exactly the event amount (718.6 USDC in the table), the account's shares are 0, the allowance is 0, every other account's shares are unchanged, the payment alert carries "Read-back ok", the next evaluation reads `NO_DEBT` and the default alert resolves.
- **Hostile states with a confirmed default** (the keeper's state says confirmed, the account really is exhausted): stale AVAX price, zero answer, manipulated oracle (divergence), pool `observe()` reverting, pool liquidity below the floor: **no transaction, not even an approval.**
- **Budget refusals before any spend**: daily cap, fee above the cap, AVAX below the floor, USDC not covering the debt: no transaction, a critical alert.
- **Failure paths**: simulation fails after the approval (a drained balance) -> allowance reset to 0, nothing paid, cooldown; the repay transaction fails after the simulation passed -> allowance reset attempted and the keeper halts; a pending marker at start-up or a halted state stops everything; a signer whose address differs from `treasury.address` is refused.
- **Several defaults**: two more accounts are liquidated to exhaustion; with the cap at 1,500, the keeper pays the largest debts first, the cap stops the rest and alerts (2 payments in 24 h, 1,437 USDC).
- **Unrecoverable debt** (operator definition, `src/backing.ts`): `sum over borrowers of max(0, debt - collateral/LI) - reserves`, clipped at 0, reported with a completeness flag. Zero on the healthy and recoverable rows (stages 1-2), positive on the tail and default rows; replaces the ambiguous "claims minus cash" alert. Unit-tested separately.
- **Mutants**: seven keeper mutants (no re-verification, confirmation always true, no cap, unlimited allowance, wrong account, halt ignored, reference status not required) are each killed (`source-mutations.txt`).
Bugs the tests found in my own first draft, fixed before this report: the ABI of the keeper lacked `repayBorrowBehalf` and `borrowBalanceStored` (a payment would have been mined and then the read-back would have thrown, halting after the fact); the monitor's outflow check did not see the keeper's ledger. A unit test now checks that every function the keeper encodes is in its ABI.

## 5. What it cannot protect against (read before funding it)
- **A compromised host with the password**: the keystore file and the password in the host's secret store are both on the host; an attacker with both can sign anything. The daily cap is **software**, it does not bind an attacker. The limits are the wallet's balance (keep only the protection capital there) and the tripwire above (detects, does not prevent). Safe top-ups should replenish only what is spent.
- **A wrong-but-agreeing oracle and reference** (simultaneous manipulation) could produce a false `DEFAULT_EXHAUSTED`: the loss is then the debt of an account that liquidators could still have partly recovered, bounded by the cap. Not covered by any test; a USDC de-peg is not covered either.
- The keeper pays the whole debt of a defaulted account; it never pays a partial amount, never pauses anything, never unpauses, and has no admin rights.
- The confirmation window delays a payment by at least `confirmSeconds`, the TWAP gate can hold it up to about 30 minutes after a fast move (treasury document 2.3.1).
- Not tested against the real chain: gas behaviour on a congested network, nonce handling after a dropped transaction (the keeper sends strictly in sequence and waits for each receipt), the real Safe top-up flow (helper not built), the real Telegram.
