## 2. Treasury `repayBorrowBehalf` procedure (qualified, no funds committed; detail in `avalanche-cash-only-treasury-repayment.md`)
- **Roles:** monitor (reads only, no funds); treasury multisig (holds the protection USDC and gas only, no admin power; `repayBorrowBehalf` is permissionless so authorization = who holds the funds); two liquidator operators (separate keys, each holding the inventory above); admin/guardian separate. The Ledger `0x064eFE76812F5f18Bd1aA9708739Fb33C810B2dD` is the intended initial admin/signer (the script is run with `--ledger --sender`).
- **Detection rule:** repay only when all oracle and reference prices are non-zero, the account is in shortfall, debt > 0, collateral value x 1e18 < debt value x LI (a full liquidation cannot clear it) and collateral <= USD 0.01 (liquidation exhausted); amount = the debt at the repay transaction (`type(uint256).max` sentinel).
- **Timings (proposals, not derived from tests):** repay within 1 hour of detection; escalate at 4 hours; outer bound 24 hours. Interest on the unrecoverable debt is small (at most 0.52% of the loss after 30 days); the real exposure is the first-come-first-served exit window while unrepaid.
- **If under-funded:** the last suppliers in the withdrawal queue absorb the loss; each USDC repaid removes exactly one USDC of shortfall; late funding heals; at tails of -67.6% or deeper some supplier recovers 0% even at 50% funding.

## 4. Reference price (monitor-side, independent of Chainlink; fails closed). **Selected by the operator: Uniswap V3 WAVAX/USDC 0.05% 30-minute TWAP. Pyth is dropped.**
Full specification: `avalanche-cash-only-treasury-repayment.md` section 2.3.1. In short: pool `0xfAe3...c45E` re-derived from the factory; `observe([1800, 0])` at the same block as the controller read; USD via the oracle's own USDC price (**a USDC de-peg is not covered**; the guarded oracle has no peg guard). **Tolerance 500 bp** between oracle and reference, from a 14-day sample at 10-minute spacing (2,013 points: median gap 15 bp, p99.9 190 bp, maximum 308 bp; a short sample, not a bound). Beyond it the verdict is `PRICE_DIVERGENCE` and nothing is paid; a missing, reverting, zero, wrong-pool or low-liquidity reference gives `NO_REFERENCE_PRICE`, also nothing paid. A wrong reference can only delay a payment, never cause one (tested); its real job is to flag an oracle failure (page the operator, with pausing borrowing as a human decision). The TWAP lags a fast move by up to about 30 minutes, so the treasury can be held back for that long. Cost: no subscription, no gas. Tested: the tolerance to the wei in both directions, the wrong-reference property, three mutations (16 treasury tests; M3 removes the gate and 2 tests fail). Not tested: simultaneous manipulation of both sources, the liquidity-floor value, provider reliability.
