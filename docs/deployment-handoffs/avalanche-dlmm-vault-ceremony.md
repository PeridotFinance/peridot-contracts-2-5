## 6. Stage 2: open the vault and fund it (Ledger through the Safe), OPERATOR-ONLY

**Why one atomic transaction (W10 F1).** The vault starts paused and `deposit` is `whenNotPaused`. If `unpause` and the first deposit were separate transactions, a window would exist in which the vault is open with a supply of zero, and a dust-supply inflation attack (4 share-wei plus a donation: DERIVED loss up to USD 17 of a depositor) could be set up against the first real depositor. W2 added a deposit-side minimum supply (`SupplyBelowMinimum`: a deposit may not leave `totalSupply` in (0, 1e6)), which makes a seed below 1e6 impossible in any case. The ceremony therefore opens the vault with ONE Safe MultiSend: `[ vault.unpause(), USDC.approve(vault, SEED), vault.deposit(SEED, SEED_RECEIVER) ]`, `SEED >= 1000000` (USD 1), and the seed is never redeemed.

**Who pays: the Safe.** The Safe delegatecalls MultiSendCallOnly, so `msg.sender` of all three inner calls is the Safe: the seed USDC must sit in the Safe first (step 2a).

**Where the seed shares go: `0x000000000000000000000000000000000000dEaD` (recommended), not the Safe and not the Ledger.** (1) "Never redeemed" becomes a property of the chain instead of a promise: nobody holds a key for `0xdEaD`, whereas the Safe or the Ledger could redeem it by one mistaken or hostile signature (section 8 lists "get the owner's own money out" as a normal action; a redeem of the seed would reopen F1); (2) the read-back `balanceOf(0xdEaD) >= SEED` is a clean invariant, separate from the Ledger's working position; (3) the cost is USD 1 that can never be withdrawn (it stays in the bins and counts toward the cap). If the operator prefers, `SEED_RECEIVER=$SAFE` works the same way but a Safe transaction can then redeem it. **Rehearsed with `0xdEaD`.** On the fork only, `0xdEaD` was impersonated at the very end to redeem the seed and prove the vault empties completely (it did).

## 9. What is NOT done by agents

Agents do not: generate or hold the throwaway key or the keeper key; fund anything; sign or send any transaction in stages 1, 2, 3 or the brakes of section 8; touch the Ledger; run any `live` mode; start, stop or configure the keeper host, the firewall or the droplets; delete the deployer key; set or print an RPC URL; edit the Safe. Agents prepare documents, scripts and rehearsals on a local fork with impersonated accounts and nothing real (the rehearsal's Ledger/Safe/`0xdEaD` impersonation, balance writes and mock feed code exist only on the local anvil).

## 10. Questions of the skeleton: what the code and the rehearsal resolved, and what remains open

- **A. Rebalancer and when**: the keeper wallet is `InitParams.rebalancer`, set inside `initialize`; there is no `setRebalancer` step. RESOLVED.
- **B. Window between creation and verification**: the vault is created PAUSED by `initialize`; the Safe unpauses it inside the one MultiSend (section 6). RESOLVED.
- **C. First funding**: the seed through the MultiSend (paid by the Safe, the USDC moved there by the Ledger first), the Ledger's own deposit afterwards, `openBand` by the keeper wallet (`dlmm-keeper open-band --send`, the only rebalancer-role call in the ceremony). Deposits before `openBand` are idle by design. RESOLVED.
- **D. ProxyAdmin ownership**: owned by the Safe from the first instruction (`initialOwner` = Safe); no hand-over. RESOLVED.
- **E. Zero cap**: closed, not unlimited (MEASURED). Initial `paused` = true. RESOLVED.
- **G. Exit-probe monitoring**: the keeper probes every 20 cycles and blocks re-centring on a failure; nothing pauses the vault automatically: `pause()` is a Safe (Ledger) transaction. OPEN (operator decision whether a separate monitor is needed).
- **F. Script and ABI names**: final. Still OPEN: no human review of the W2 fixes of `3726773c` (the rehearsal ran on them); `deploy-resume` unverified; the pinned MultiSendCallOnly hash was taken from a public file, not from a second independent source; the contract was only exercised on a fork (anvil's default hardfork, not necessarily Avalanche's active one, while the build targets osaka).
