## 2. Question 1: where the swap fees go (MEASURED at the pin; source = Pharaoh `DLMMPool.sol`, `DLMMFactory.sol`, `DLMMFeeCollector.sol`, `Voter`/`VoterDLMMActions.sol`, `DLMMRewarder`, verified on Routescan)

- **Swap fee split.** In `DLMMPool.swap`, per bin: `totalFees` is taken out of the input; `pFees = totalFees x protocolShare / 10,000`; the rest stays in the bin reserves (`_bins[id] = reserves + amountsIn - pFees - out`). **LP share = 1 - protocolShare, accrues inside the bin (compounds into bin liquidity), and is claimed only by burning bin shares** (`removeLiquidity` or `pair.burn`). No separate fee claim exists.
- **Chain read at the pin (`data/pinned_reads.txt`):** `getStaticFeeParameters` = baseFactor 2500, filterPeriod 10 s, decayPeriod 120 s, reductionFactor 5000, variableFeeControl 2,000,000, **protocolShare 5000 (50 %)**, maxVolatilityAccumulator 100,000. binStep 1 (0.01 %). `getVariableFeeParameters`: volatilityAccumulator 0, volatilityReference 0, idReference 8388598, timeOfLastUpdate 1794384227. Base fee = `baseFactor x binStep x 1e10 / 1e18` = 0.0025 %; maximum variable fee = `(maxVolAcc x binStep)^2 x control / 100 / 1e18` = 0.02 %.
- **Reconciled with events:** protocol fees / total fees = 50.0 % in every epoch (e.g. epoch 2958: 66.6 of 133.2 USD); total fees / volume = 0.255, 0.255, 0.359, 0.267, 0.251, 0.251, 0.251, 0.258 bp (epochs 2958 to 2965), i.e. the base fee plus a little variable fee (epoch 2960).
- **Where the protocol half goes:** pair `collectProtocolFees` is callable only by the factory's fee recipient = `DLMMFeeCollector` 0x684b...289c; it sends to the pool's `feeDistributor` (veVoters) after a treasury cut; `treasuryFees` = 0 at the pin, so **100 % of the protocol half goes to voters** (`FeesCollected` path); `voter.gaugeForPool(pool)` = the rewarder 0x32E6...aDC and `isAlive` = true. Flash-loan fees go 100 % to protocol (37 events).
- **The 50 % is not the factory constant and is governance-set.** The factory constants are default 2500 and "gauged" 10000. The pool's value 5000 is a manual setting. `setPoolProtocolShare` may be called by the factory owner (0x3176...f483, also the `accessHub`) or the voter at any value up to 10,000 with no timelock in the source. **Measured history (`StaticFeeParametersSet` events):** share 2500 (13 May) -> 500 (30 May) -> **10000 on 3 Jun when the rewarder/gauge was created (LPs got 0 fees)** -> 500 (19 Jun) -> **5000 (16 Aug 02:57 UTC)**, constant through the 8-epoch window. baseFactor history: 20000 (13 May), 25 (15 May), 250 and 2500 (30 May 21:31).
- **Oracle:** `getOracleParameters` at the pin returns sampleLifetime 120, **size 0**, activeSize 0: the pool has no TWAP oracle (the vault's R3 `observe` guard has no DLMM equivalent until someone calls the permissionless `increaseOracleLength`).

## 7. Question 7: claim rules, forfeiture, staking

Verified source of the **actual beacon implementation** 0x9b9d...CEb2 (the beacon, `DLMMRewarderFactory` 0xd284...548C, returns it at the pin). Note: the Routescan verified bundle of the rewarder PROXY contains an older source that differs (it reads `timeThresholdForRewarder()` and has no events); the implementation reads `voter.timeThresholdForGauge(rewarder)`. Both thresholds read 111 at the pin. A pin of the implementation codehash is required in any vault guard.

- **No staking, no gauge deposit/withdraw.** LB shares are an ERC-1155-like token held by the LP in the pair (`balanceOf(user, id)`). The rewarder is installed as the pair's hook (`HooksParametersSet`, 3 Jun, flags BEFORE_SWAP, BEFORE_MINT, BEFORE_BURN, BEFORE_TRANSFER = 0x151) and settles on every swap, mint and burn. `batchTransferFrom` reverts for any real transfer ("transfers disabled"): shares cannot be moved, wrapped or staked elsewhere.
- **Accrual.** `rewardsPerSecond x dt` (the weekly voter notification sets `rewardsPerSecond = ceil(remaining / 7 days)`, `notifyRewarder`) goes to the bins in `[active + deltaBinA, active + deltaBinB)`, weighted by bin liquidity and shared per bin by share balance. **Today the range is `(0, 1)`: the active bin only** (MEASURED at all samples; rewards accrue only if that bin has liquidity, otherwise they stay in the rewarder and roll into the next notification). Governance (`setDeltaBins`, up to 11 bins) can change this with no event.
- **Claim.** `claim(user, ids)` with `user == msg.sender`, the rewarder must still be the linked hook, settles the listed bins into `unclaimedRewards[user]` and transfers PHAR (never xPHAR; the DLMM gauge pays PHAR only). The caller must list every bin it still holds; bins already burned were settled at the burn. `getPendingRewards(user, ids)` is a view. A third party cannot claim for the vault.
- **Forfeiture.** `_beforeMint` and `_beforeBurn` call `_updateUser` then `_markModified(user, id)`; `claim` calls `_updateUser` too. `_shouldForfeit` is true if anti-sybil is on (MEASURED true) and `now - lastModified(user, bin) <= timeThresholdForGauge` (MEASURED 111 s). Then **only the rewards accrued on that bin since the user's last settlement are forfeited**, they are released (`_releaseRewards`: stay in the rewarder and are re-notified in a later week) and not sent to a treasury. Versus the CL gauge (`avalanche-vault-phar-decisions.md`): CL slashes at the same 111 s but forfeits the whole unclaimed backlog of earlier periods and routes it to r33 or the treasury; here a touch inside 111 s loses at most 111 s of accrual on one bin (the whole bin's accrual over 111 s at the current rate is about 1 PHAR, USD 0.05; a 1 % share loses USD 0.0005) and no backlog exists because each touch settles. No vesting, no xPHAR exit penalty path.
- **Does an unstaked LP earn?** There is nothing to stake. A position earns fees whenever its bins trade, and PHAR only while its bin is the active bin.
- **Hook liveness and principal exit (unlike the CL position manager).** The pair calls the hook through `Hooks._safeCall`, which **reverts the whole pair call if the hook call fails** (any revert, or a wrong return selector). `beforeBurn`, `beforeMint` and `beforeSwap` are all hooked, so a rewarder or voter that reverts (an upgrade through the beacon or the voter proxy, a changed threshold function, a broken `isAntiSybilEnabled`/`timeThresholdForGauge` read) blocks `burn`, hence `removeLiquidity`, `emergencyExit` and `redeemInKind`, until the factory owner removes the hook (`removeLBHooksOnPair`). Transfers are also disabled, so there is no other exit. This is a real difference from `avalanche-vault-phar-decisions.md` ("principal itself still exits"): **principal exit depends on the factory owner/accessHub's discretion and the voter's liveness**. MEASURED: today a burn is hooked (flags 0x151 include BEFORE_BURN).
- **Mint recipient rule.** `_checkMintRecipient`: `sender == to` or `voter.isAuthorizedDLMMManager(sender)`. The Pharaoh `DLMMRouter` 0xff2b...5d93d is authorised (MEASURED true at the pin); governance can remove it.
- **Governance levers in the verified source (none behind a timelock that I could see):** `setPoolProtocolShare` (owner or voter, to 100 %), `setDeltaBins`, `setTimeThresholdForGauge`, anti-sybil switch, `removeLBHooksOnPair` / `setLBHooksParametersOnPair` (claims then revert "unlinked", `setRewardPerSecond` reverts "stopped"), `sweep(token, to)` (any token; the reward token too once the hook is unlinked, while `claim` then reverts, so unclaimed PHAR is at risk), beacon `setImplementation` by `accessHub`, `removeAuthorizedDLMMManager`. Identity and delay of the owner/accessHub 0x3176...f483 (a small proxy contract) are **n/a** (follow-up: read its implementation and owners at the pin).

## 8. Question 8: interface changes, what carries over, engineer-days, risks

**What exists today and what it cannot do.** `LFJLBRouterAdapter` (contracts/contracts/margin, 194 lines) is a swap-only adapter: it speaks `swapExactTokensForTokens(amountIn, amountOutMin, Path{pairBinSteps, versions, tokenPath}, to, deadline)` of an LFJ router with route and operator allow-lists. It has **no addLiquidity or removeLiquidity**. **Main risks.** (1) **Valuation of bin positions:** composition is real reserves, so NAV needs only oracle prices, but any swap in the band changes the vault's X/Y mix: an attacker can shift composition by up to the band's value (about 0.07 % of V at +-3 bins; 0.29 % worst seen peg deviation). (2) **Oracle/peg for NAV and guards:** the pool has no oracle (size 0); the guard must use the Chainlink USDC/USDt feeds (R3's `priceOracle`) or a one-time `increaseOracleLength` plus warm-up. (3) **Manipulation of the active bin:** the active bin is the only rewarded bin and holds USD 9k to 2M depending on the whales; a USD 10 swap moved the price to -0.30 % on 30 Oct. A flash-style swap can move the active bin out of the vault's band between a keeper's read and its mint: use `activeIdDesired` and `idSlippage`. Reward capture is gameable by anyone who parks size in the active bin (not a vault loss, a dilution). (4) **Bin-rebalance cost:** gas is negligible (MEASURED on this pool: median 370k gas per deposit tx and 258k per withdraw at 3.5 gwei median, about USD 0.01 each at AVAX 8.58); swap fee on the converted half is at most 0.0225 % x V/2 (USD 0.23 at V = 2,000), so the cost is the adverse selection already in section 4, not the mechanics. (5) **Governance and upgrade risk:** section 7 (the 50 % LP share has changed four times in 6 months; the rewarder is a beacon proxy upgradable by `accessHub`; the router can be de-authorised). (6) **Whale liquidity:** two addresses hold 97 % (replayed LB supply equals the chain's `totalSupply` for all 25 bins read at the pin). (7) **Exit liveness:** a failing rewarder or voter blocks `burn` (section 7). (8) **Verification risk:** the Routescan proxy bundle differs from the implementation, pin the implementation codehash.

## 10. Caveats and data gaps (the five most important first)

1. **Income is a regime artefact.** 94 % of the 8-week gauge PHAR (19.1k of 20.2k) comes from the last four epochs after emissions rose from under 450 to 4.5k to 9.6k PHAR a week; the median over 8 epochs (0.89 to 0.96) and the last four (3.2 to 3.4) differ 3.6x. The emission is a voter decision that can fall to zero (no transfer in four of the last 23 weeks).
2. **The reward goes only to the active bin and the active-bin liquidity swings from USD 1 to 2M**; per-USD-1,000 income depends on where two whales sit; 97 % of liquidity is two LP addresses and 73 % to 85 % of volume is calls of USD 100k and more.
3. **Reference-price noise in adverse selection:** parity, Llama and markout references disagree in sign at pool level (+6,968 / -2,400 / -1,186 USD over 8 epochs); the gross figures are noise-inflated; the markout is the most defensible but is a 1-hour horizon. **n/a:** a synchronous per-swap market price (a Chainlink USDC/USDT feed read at each swap block) would close this.
4. **Not run:** no fork test, no transaction and no simulation: the claim path, router ABI compatibility with `LFJLBRouterAdapter`, and the distribution encoding are from source reading. The PHAR/WAVAX concentrated pool was not quoted; PHAR was valued at the pin price for all epochs.
5. **Governance identity and delays are n/a** (owner/accessHub 0x3176...f483); the 50 % LP share, the rewarded range and the hook can all change in one call (the protocol share changed four times since launch), and a failing hook blocks `burn`, so principal exit is not unconditional.

Other: Gecko/DexScreener TVL (USD 3.74M) equals the replayed pin value (USD 3.745M). Per-epoch observations cover 100 % of each epoch except 2966 (92 %). Sampling of in-range liquidity at the 32 archive blocks is only for reconciliation; the reported liquidity is the full replay. The "per-USD-1,000" figures use a marginal USD 1,000 position and ignore that a real vault touch within 111 s forfeits up to 111 s of accrual (negligible).
