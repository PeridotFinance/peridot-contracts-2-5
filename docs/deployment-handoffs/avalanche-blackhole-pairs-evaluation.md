## 2. Method: what "in-range liquidity" means and how the weekly series were built

- **Epochs (MEASURED).** Blackhole: `BlackTimeLibrary.WEEK = 7 days` on the unix clock, i.e. Thursday 00:00 UTC; the docs say the same; each gauge got exactly one `RewardAdded` about 1 to 2 minutes after the flip in all 14 epochs read (Routescan logs, `data/bh_reward_added.json`). Pharaoh: `GaugeV3` indexes `tokenTotalSupplyByPeriod[timestamp / WEEK]`, the same Thursday boundary; period 2965 starts 29 Oct 00:00 UTC. The 8 epochs are periods 2958 to 2965.
- **Gauge emission** per epoch: Blackhole = sum of `RewardAdded(uint256)` events of the gauge in the epoch (events, not `rewardRate x DURATION`, so there is no leftover roll-over error); Pharaoh = `tokenTotalSupplyByPeriod(period, PHAR)` read at the pin block (`data/ph_emissions.json`); xPHAR was zero in every period of both gauges.
- **In-range liquidity (denominator).** Position under test: USD 1,000 in a **+-0.1 % price band centred on the pool price** (+-10 ticks on spacing-1 pools; all four pools are spacing 1). Its liquidity `L_m = 1000 / v` where `v` is the USD value per unit of L of that band at the sampled price. The competing liquidity is the pool's own active figure at the sampled block: for Blackhole the **farmed in-range liquidity** `gauge.totalSupply()` (the virtual pool's `currentLiquidity`; unstaked liquidity earns nothing, so it is not in the denominator); for Pharaoh `pool.liquidity()` (all in-range liquidity earns). Marginal share per sample = `L_m / (L_active + L_m)`; the epoch value is the mean over the samples in that epoch (**3 or 4 samples per epoch**, every 2 days at 12:00 UTC, 31 archive samples plus the pin, `data/samples.json`); tokens per USD 1,000 = epoch emission x mean share. This includes our own dilution, but it is an **upper bound**: the band is assumed re-centred and in range at every sample (the Blackhole WBTC/BTC.b pool price sat more than 0.10 % off parity 25 % of the time, so a fixed +-0.1 % band would often have been out of range and earned nothing). The "headline pro rata" figure (emission / existing in-range USD) is in `data/analysis.json` but is not used: in weeks when almost all staked liquidity had left (BLACK pools, 22 Oct to 4 Nov: in-range farmed liquidity fell to USD 1.1k to 18.7k band-equivalent for WBTC/BTC.b) it explodes, and the marginal figure correctly tops out at the gauge total.
- **Sale price.** Per-token USD = the realised price from the pair's own `getAmountOut` at the pin (table row), applied to the epoch tokens. The USD 500 and USD 2,000 sales are **50 to 5,000 times the weekly emission of these gauges** (USD 0.4 to USD 9 a week for the whole gauge), so they bound the price from below; the fee-only quote for the real weekly amounts is the last figure in that row. The deepest market for BLACK is a Blackhole v2 vAMM (BLACK/USDC with USD 35.5k per side, BLACK/WAVAX is shallower: USD 500 gives -2.32 %, USD 2,000 gives -7.12 %; SUPER/BLACK at USD 220k reserve is a different asset, not a sale into dollars). For PHAR the deepest dollar route is the legacy vAMM PHAR/WAVAX; a concentrated PHAR/WAVAX 0.025 % pool (USD 101k) exists and was **not quoted** (n/a: I did not locate a quoter; the vAMM figure is the conservative one for large size, the CL pool could be better for the small real amounts).
- **What 8 weeks cannot show.** Reward token prices moved a lot (Llama 4-hour points: BLACK 0.0019, then 0.0041 mid-window, now 0.0024; PHAR 0.021, then 0.069, now 0.046); the per-week series are dominated by who staked or left (BLACK pools: in-range farmed liquidity moved by a factor of 100 within the window), so medians are quoted next to means. Eight weekly points per pool is a sample of 8; sampling every 2 days misses intraday liquidity moves.

## 4. Arbitrage loss (measure 3)

**Data (MEASURED):** every `Swap` event of each pool from 6 Aug to 11 Nov 2026 from the Routescan public log API (`data/swaps_*.jsonl`: 1,226 / 4,248 / 11,351 / 12,251 swaps); the loss window is the 8 epochs above.

**Method (DERIVED).** Per swap: `pnl = value received - value paid` at a reference price at the swap time (the event amounts include the fee the taker paid). Fee `= amountIn x pool fee`. **`LVR_i = fee_i - pnl_i`**: what the taker extracted beyond the fee they paid; positive means the LP lost. "Gross adverse" is the sum of the positive `LVR_i`; "net" is the signed sum. Attribution to a USD 1,000 +-0.1 % position: `LVR_i x L_m / (L_i + L_m)` with `L_i` the pool's liquidity after the swap (as in the event). Two references: **parity** (1:1 for the three correlated pairs, the exchange rate for sAVAX/WAVAX), the upper bound that counts any deviation from peg as loss if the peg is restored; **market** (Llama 4-hourly prices, nearest earlier point). LP fee income: Blackhole 0; Pharaoh 75 % of the fee (pool `feeProtocol` = 250000 of 1,000,000, source `RamsesV3Pool.sol` line 538 on Sourcify, read at the pin).

**Caveats that matter.** (1) The share formula overstates when a swap crosses our band: a USD 1,000 +-0.1 % position cannot lose more than about its band width (0.2 %, USD 2) in one swap, so the attribution is **capped at USD 2.00 per swap** (`CAP` in `s9_analysis.py`; the first version of this analysis had no cap and produced a USD 9.87 figure that is not physically possible, now removed); the pool-level number is exact for the pool. (2) The reference noise (Llama prices have non-synchronous sources) is larger than the 0.5 to 1 bp fees of the stable pools, so the **net** column carries sign noise; read the gross column as an upper bound and the table's worst-swap rows as events. (3) For sAVAX/WAVAX the Llama price for sAVAX is not usable (it disagreed with the contract rate by up to 59 % at one 4-hour point) so both references are the exchange rate; the pool trades at a market price near, not at, the rate, which biases gross up and net down; **n/a for a clean market-referenced LVR**; a follow-up needs an independent sAVAX/USD feed. (4) The "worst episode" for the correlated pairs is a peg-deviation episode: see the peg row (Blackhole WBTC/BTC.b 21 Oct, Pharaoh sAVAX/WAVAX 3 Nov).

## 6. Claim rules, forfeiture, vesting (measure 6)

Blackhole, from verified source (Routescan, all [S]): `GaugeCL`, `GaugeManager` implementation `0x66c6...4091` (proxy `0x59aa...CBf6`), `AlgebraEternalFarming` `0x01A8...271A`, `FarmingCenter` `0xa47A...A449`, NFPM `0x3fED...2146`.

- **No claim window, no early-claim forfeiture, no vesting** exists in these contracts: `AlgebraEternalFarming.collectRewards` (lines 337 to 364) credits `rewards[owner]`; `_claimReward` (469 to 483) pays any amount at any time; there is no timestamp or lock check. Epoch emissions arrive weekly via `GaugeManager.distribute` -> `GaugeCL.notifyRewardAmount` -> `addRewards` and `setRates` (one notify about 1 to 2 minutes after each Thursday 00:00 UTC, MEASURED).
- **Who can claim, and the path a vault should use** (source reading, not executed; no fork run was allowed): `FarmingCenter.collectRewards(key, tokenId)` needs owner or approved and credits `rewards[ownerOf(tokenId)]`; `FarmingCenter.claimReward(token, to, amount)` debits `rewards[msg.sender]`. `GaugeCL.getReward` is `onlyDistribution` and calls `farmingCenter.claimReward(...)` with the **gauge** as `msg.sender`, so `GaugeManager.claimRewards(gauge, ids, bonus)` appears to debit the gauge's own balance, not the NFT owner's (inference). On chain, the first 1,000 `RewardClaimed` events on the farming contract from block 96.0M (to 24 Sep 2026) come mostly from a few non-gauge addresses (the top one has no `poolAddress`, so it is not a gauge) that pay other recipients, and **zero** `RewardClaimed` events exist with either of our two gauges as `from` (blocks 65M to 100M). So the vault (as NFT owner) must call the FarmingCenter directly. Liquidity changes on a farmed NFT (`increase` / `decreaseLiquidity`) call `FarmingCenter.applyLiquidityDelta`, which exits and re-enters the farm; rewards earned so far move to `rewards[owner]`. That call is inside a `try/catch` in the NFPM that only emits `FarmingFailed`; **an NFT can silently drop out of the farm** (operational risk; a vault would need a staked-state check each rebalance).
- **Governance and failure modes** [S]: `setEmergencyWithdrawStatus` (administrator) makes exits skip reward accounting (rewards of the open interval are not credited); `deactivateIncentive` and `decreaseRewardsAmount` exist; `GaugeCL.activateEmergencyMode` blocks `deposit`, `withdraw` and notify; `GaugeManager.killGauge` stops future distribution. [C] The incentive keys have `nonce` 5 (WBTC/BTC.b) and 9 (AUSD/USDC) at the pin, i.e. the incentive was re-created that many times since the pools were launched; no `EternalFarmingCreated` or `IncentiveDeactivated` event appears in blocks 88M to 100M (about the last 4.5 months). [C] `minimalPositionWidth` of both incentives = 1 tick, so a +-10 tick position may farm. These are admin powers over rewards, comparable in kind to Pharaoh's governance surface (U1 in `avalanche-vault-phar-decisions.md`).
- **Pharaoh's rule for comparison** (from `avalanche-vault-phar-decisions.md` and the proposal): rewards are slashed if `now - lastModified <= 111 s` on a liquidity change or claim; the slash forfeits the backlog; xPHAR exit costs 50 % but `xRatio` is 0. **The Blackhole equivalent is absent**: the entitlement ledger, the 111 s guard and the xPHAR exit option are not needed for Blackhole; the equivalent obligations are re-staking and the farm-state check above.

## 7. Does the Algebra position manager fit our vault code? (measure 7)

**There is no "adapter interface" to implement.** `IBoostedYieldAdapter.sol` (38 lines, `deposit/withdraw/totalUnderlying`) is the BoostedPErc20 yield-adapter interface (used by `UpshiftAdapter`), not the Pharaoh vault's interface. The Pharaoh vaults are the standalone ERC-4626 vault `PharaohLiquidityVaultR3` (858 lines) plus `PharaohRewardExtensionR3` (103 lines) in `contracts/vault-r3/src/r3/`, written against four hand-made interfaces under `orig/interfaces/pharaoh/` (`IPharaohPositionManager` 81, `IPharaohSwapRouter` 20, `IPharaohPool` 31, `IPharaohFactory` 8 lines). `PharaohCLRouterAdapter` (119 lines) is the margin swap adapter, with the same interfaces. A Blackhole vault is therefore a **new vault variant**, not a new adapter in the `IBoostedYieldAdapter` sense.

## 8. The five most important caveats and data gaps

1. **Reward tokens are nearly worthless, so everything is small.** BLACK is USD 0.0024 and PHAR USD 0.046; the whole Blackhole gauges pay USD 4.0 and USD 2.1 a week. Any verdict on the rule's R1 is a statement about token price as much as about liquidity; a recovery of BLACK or of votes changes the answer by an order of magnitude.
2. **Only 8 weekly points and 3 to 4 liquidity samples per epoch.** In-range farmed liquidity in the two BLACK pools moved by 100 x in the window (WBTC/BTC.b band-equivalent USD 1.1k to USD 137k), so the per-USD-1,000 series are noisy; medians and means are both shown.
3. **Reference-price noise in the arbitrage-loss column.** Llama 4-hour aggregate prices are non-synchronous and were unusable for sAVAX (up to 59 % off the contract rate in one point; and its WBTC vs BTC.b ratio differs from the pool by about 0.6 % on 5 Nov when the pool was at -0.06 %). The signed "net" LVR is not reliable for the stable pairs; the gross and the worst-swap rows are the conservative reading. A follow-up needs a synchronous oracle (Chainlink where it exists, or Pharaoh/Trader Joe pool prices at the same block) per swap.
4. **Dust trades dominate the "worst deviation" of thin pools.** AUSD/USDC +1.24 % is a USD 5.40 swap and Pharaoh USDC/USDt -0.82 % a USD 18 swap; the table also gives the worst for swaps of at least USD 1,000. The one real liquidity event is the 21 Aug 2026 vacuum of Pharaoh USDC/USDt (15 s at the tick bound). **Finding for our existing vault:** the Pharaoh USDC/USDt pool holds only USD 1.3k TVL today and its 1 % depth is USD 174 (USDt in) and USD 1,137 (USDC in), so a USD 200 vault exit is about the whole depth of that side.
5. **Not verified or not run.** The claim path (FarmingCenter direct) and the NFT-drops-out-of-farm risk are from source reading; no transaction, fork or simulation was run (rules: no fork jobs, no signing). Pharaoh's concentrated PHAR/WAVAX pool was not quoted. No V1.9 pool was examined. The Blackhole Voter, Minter and BLACK schedule contracts were not read beyond `GaugeManager` and the emission events; the docs' schedule (phase 2: -1 % per epoch) matches the measured BLACK series for the WBTC gauge (1903 to 1590).
