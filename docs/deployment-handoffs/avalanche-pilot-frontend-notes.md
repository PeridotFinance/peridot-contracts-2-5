# Avalanche pilot: notes for the frontend (lending markets and the two Pharaoh vaults)

Status: notes for whoever builds the screens; every number is from a FORK rehearsal or a read-only chain read on 2026-11-06 and is labelled. The frontend is password-gated but the **contracts are public**: any address can deposit or borrow directly, so the on-chain caps are the real limits and the screens must display them, not enforce them.

## 1. Vault deposit screen (Pharaoh USDC vault and Pharaoh WAVAX vault): show an EXIT COST
- **Show it as an exit cost on the deposit screen, before the user confirms:** *an immediate deposit-and-withdraw returns about **99.46%** of the USDC (cost about 0.54%) and about **98.77%** of the WAVAX (cost about 1.23%)*. Source: fork rehearsal at mainnet block 99,580,846 (`operator/scripts/rehearse-vault-opening.ts`, report `avalanche-cash-only-operator-evidence/vault-opening-rehearsal.json`): a deposit up to the cap, then `deployIdle()`, then an immediate redeem of all shares: USDC 4,970.16 deposited and 4,943.10 returned (54 bp), WAVAX 593.23 deposited and 585.88 returned (123 bp).
- Label it honestly: *"Not a fee paid to Peridot: the cost of entering and leaving the Pharaoh liquidity position (swaps, pool fee, price impact) and the vault's conservative valuation. It is not earned back by waiting a short time; yield accrues over time."* Say that the figure is a measurement of a near-cap deposit at one moment and that it varies with the pool.
- **Do not compute the cost from `previewDeposit`/`previewRedeem`:** on 2026-11-06 the previews gave a much more pessimistic round trip (about 97.0% for the USDC vault on a USD 1,000 deposit, about 91.0% for the WAVAX vault on 100 WAVAX) because they apply the vault's haircut (1% and 3%) differently from the actual mint; they are not what a deposit actually costs. The deposit mint rule changes with the R3 upgrade (13 to 15 Nov): the exit-cost figure must be **re-measured after R3** and the screen updated.
- **Show the cap and the remaining room:** `depositCap()` and `totalAssets()`; the room is `depositCap - totalAssets`. **`maxDeposit()` can overstate the room by a few units** (3 units of USDC in the rehearsal): never build the maximum deposit from it to the last unit; leave a margin (0.01%) or let the transaction fail gracefully with the cap message (`Vault__DepositCapExceeded`).
- Deposits go in idle and **earn nothing until the rebalancer calls `deployIdle()`** (the Ledger does it on 12 Nov for the team seed; automation follows with the rebalancer module). Show the deposited amount as "waiting to be deployed" until then (read the vault's idle balance).
- **Gas: never send a vault transaction with the raw gas estimate.** `deposit`, `redeem`, `deployIdle` use a few percent more gas when mined in a later block than at the estimate; with a raw estimate the transaction is mined and reverts out of gas (reproduced, vault-opening document section 4). With ethers v5 or viem add at least 30% to the estimate (or set a fixed limit: 4,000,000 for a first deposit into a position, 2,000,000 for the others). Wallets that pad the estimate (MetaMask does) are less exposed but not immune.
- A deposit or a withdrawal can **revert when the pool price is away from its TWAP** (price/oracle deviation guards): show a "try again later" message for `Vault__PriceDeviation` and `Vault__OracleDeviation`, not a generic error.

## 2. Lending screens (cash-only pilot, PW30)
- **USDC suppliers earn the borrow interest; WAVAX deposits earn nothing** (WAVAX borrowing is disabled): the WAVAX screen is "collateral", not "supply". Show the supply APR from the market (`supplyRatePerBlock`) and label it variable. Rehearsal measurement on the real contracts: 77.2% utilisation -> borrow APR 11.65%, supply APR 8.09%; at 50% utilisation about 3.7%. The yield depends on borrowing: with no borrower it is zero.
- Borrow limits to show: WAVAX collateral factor **30%**, USDC borrow cap **USD CAP** (the pilot's one sizing number, read from `borrowCaps`; 5,000 at the research size) for the whole market (`borrowCaps`), and the cash floor (new borrows are refused when they would take the market's cash below 20% of the supply): show "market cash available to borrow" as `min(cash - floor, cap - totalBorrows)`.
- Liquidation: a position is liquidatable when the controller reports a shortfall; the close factor is 50% and the liquidator receives a 8% incentive. Show a health indicator from `getAccountLiquidity`. Liquidations are done by the pilot's two operator bots and by anyone else.
- Interest accrues per block (about 0.94 s): refresh balances with `borrowBalanceCurrent`-style reads or compute from the borrow index.

## 3. Margin 2x (long AVAX against USDC; figures come from the margin rehearsal)
- **What it is:** long WAVAX with USDC margin and USDC debt, 2x at most, one pair (WAVAX/USDC on the Pharaoh pool). **No shorts.** Position cap **USD CAP** (margin up to about CAP/2; 5,000 and 2,500 at the research size); debt cap USD 2,500. Show the cap and refuse nothing on the screen: the contract refuses.
- **Shared budget:** margin debt is borrowed from the same USDC market as ordinary lending, so it counts against the same USD CAP borrow cap and the same cash floor. Show "USDC available to borrow" for both screens from one number. While the pilot rule holds (team lending borrows at most USD 2,000), about USD 2,450 of margin debt fits.
- **Stop-loss orders are NOT guaranteed in fast moves.** A stop-loss closes through the pool with a floor of at most 1% below the oracle price (the pair's limit; an order may ask for less, never more). In a fast move the swap can fail and the order stays open and retries; it does not execute at any price. **Liquidation is the backstop**: a long is liquidated when its equity falls below 25% of the position (about -33% in AVAX for a 2x long). Say so next to the stop-loss field, in plain words, and show the liquidation price.
- **The pool's depth is not a constant, and the screen must not quote one.** Measured on the real Pharaoh pool at two heads about two days apart (sell side, USD of WAVAX that must be sold to move the pool price down): **-1%: 182k then 106k; -5%: 554k then 207k; -10%: 777k then 231k** (block 99,670,362 vs 99,832,856; AVAX itself fell about 5% in between). Past about -10% the pool is empty on that side. So how large a fall a stop-loss (or an owner close) can be filled through depends on the hour. Do not show "your stop-loss protects you down to X". Wording for the screens and the deck: stop-loss fills depend on pool depth at the hour; funded liquidation is the mechanism.
- **Take-profit orders** have the same floor and can wait for the same reason.
- **Liquidation is not a swap into the pool.** It is done by the pilot's two operator wallets, who repay the debt and take the collateral plus a 5% bonus. If the price falls below about -50% in one step, the position is insolvent and the operators decline; the shortfall stays in the market (the monitor alerts). Do not describe liquidation as "protected".
- **Exit cost:** an immediate open-and-close of a 2x position returns about **99.81% of the margin** (cost about **0.19% of the margin**, which is about 0.1% of the position size each way). Measured by the fork rehearsal at the live head, on the real Pharaoh pool, at margin 500, 1,500 and 2,500 USDC (19 bp at all three; `avalanche-margin-evidence/`, test `test_08_roundTripCost_atThePool`). Gas of the transaction itself: about 2.4 to 2.6 million to open, 1.3 to 1.4 million to close. Show the cost on the open screen like the vault exit cost. It holds for small and normal moves only: **past about -10% the pool cannot take the sale** (section above), so an exit through the pool, including a stop-loss, can fail in a crash.
- **Orders:** an order is cancelled automatically when its position is closed or liquidated; the screen shows it as "position closed". Orders expire (at most 30 days).

## 4. Things the screens must never imply
No guarantee of yield; the pilot is capped at USD CAP per market and per vault; the contracts are not audited; the exit cost above is real; borrowing against WAVAX can be liquidated (including at a loss of the 8% incentive); margin positions can be liquidated and stop-loss orders are not guaranteed.
